## SECTION 1: OPENING & HOMEPAGE (0:00–0:30)

**[Start on HOME page - /]**

**Script:**
"Welcome to Claimly. Helping Americans claim the money they don't know about.

Right here on the homepage, users see our core mission: one sentence. One ZIP code. Instant matches to government programs they qualify for.

See that search box? That's where everything starts."

**Action:**
- Show the hero headline (animated, rotating through 5-6 different taglines)
- Point to the value propositions at the bottom: "Instant matches," "Apply right here," "Save & come back"
- Show the mission section: "Helping Americans claim the money they don't know about"

**Visual Cues:**
- Highlight the simplicity of the interface
- Point to the three trust pillars below the search
- Mention "Private by default. No SSN. Always free."

## SECTION 2: SEARCH & DISCOVERY (0:30–1:10)

**[Stay on HOME - Search]**

**Script:**
"Let me show you what happens when a real family uses Claimly.

They describe their situation: 'Single parent with 2 kids, $2,200/month income, need help with food and rent.'

Their ZIP code: Baltimore area.

Then they click 'Find help.'"

**Action:**
- Type scenario in search box: *"Single parent with 2 kids, $2,200/month income, need help with food and rent"*
- Enter ZIP: *"21218"*
- Click "Find help"
- Watch the search animation play: "Understanding your situation... Checking federal programs... Cross-referencing your ZIP code... Ranking your best matches..."

**Script (during loading):**
"In the background, our AI is using GPT-4 to:
- Parse their household situation
- Cross-reference federal eligibility rules
- Find programs available in their ZIP code
- Rank matches by likelihood"

---

## SECTION 3: RESULTS & PROGRAM CARDS (1:10–1:50)

**[Show Results section]**

**Script:**
"Seconds later, here are their matches. Notice the labels?

'Strong fit' means they're almost definitely eligible. 'Possible fit' means they should apply. 'Worth checking' means maybe.

Each card shows:
- The program name
- The estimated monthly benefit
- A personalized 'Why for you' explanation
- A button to start the application right here"

**Action:**
- Scroll through result cards slowly
- Point out the "Strong fit" badges
- Click on one strong match like **SNAP** to show the "Why for you" section
- Read it aloud: *"You have children under 18 and a household income below 130% of the federal poverty line"*
- Show buttons: "Apply here" and "Ask AI"
- Count visible programs: SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, EITC, Child Tax Credit, Emergency Rental Assistance

**Script:**
"Instead of directing them to 15 different government websites, we bring everything here. One place. One conversation."

## SECTION 4: DASHBOARD - SAVED PROGRAMS (1:50–2:15)

- Saved programs they're interested in

Below, there's a shortcut to the AI assistant for questions about any benefit."

**Action:**
- Show the three stat cards: "Saved programs" (shows count), "Active claims," "Documents"
- Point to the AI assistant banner at the bottom
- Highlight the greeting: "Welcome back [user name]"
- Show the animated cards coming in one by one

**Script:**
"This is their control center. Everything they need to track their progress toward financial help."

## SECTION 5: APPLICATION WIZARD - SMART QUESTIONS (2:15–2:55)

**[Go back to results, Click a program to open ApplyWizard]**

**Script:**
"Now let's walk through an actual application. I'll click on SNAP.

The wizard opens with an introduction—a warm, plain-English explanation of what SNAP is and what questions we need to ask."

**Action:**
- Click on SNAP card → ApplyWizard opens
- Show the intro stage with:
  - Program name and description
  - "How many questions remain?" counter
  - "Your saved information" section (if profile is filled, show pre-populated fields)
  - Progress bar
- Click "Start application"

**Script (during wizard):**
"Watch this—because they've answered these questions before, their Smart Profile already has their information. No re-entering.

Claimly only asks the questions SNAP actually needs. 4-7 questions max. No jargon. Plain English."

**Action:**
- Move through 2-3 questions:
  1. *Full name* - Already filled from Smart Profile
  2. *Household size* - Already filled
  3. *Monthly income* - Already filled (or empty if first time)
- For one question, **manually answer it** to show conversational feel
- Show the field types: text, number, ZIP, money, multiple choice, yes/no
- Point to the progress bar filling up

**Script:**
"Each question is one idea. 6th-grade reading level. Designed for real people—busy parents, elderly, anyone.

The more they apply with Claimly, the faster the next application gets, because their profile becomes more complete."

## SECTION 6: APPLICATION REVIEW & ELIGIBILITY (2:55–3:30)

**[Progress to review/preview stage]**

**Script:**
"Once all questions are answered, Claimly reviews their submission.

It checks:
- Are all required fields filled?
- Do their answers make sense together?
- What's their likely eligibility?
- What monthly benefit might they get?

Then it shows them an eligibility prediction and next steps."

**Action:**
- Show the review stage with:
  - Summary of all answers entered
  - Eligibility badge: "Likely eligible" or "Possible fit"
  - Estimated monthly benefit: e.g., "$300-$400/month"
  - Breakdown of what documents they need
  - "Next steps" section with plain-language guidance
- Point to the "Submit application" button
- Optional: Show document upload section

**Script:**
"No confusion. No hidden requirements. Just clarity: 'Here's what we think you qualify for, here's what we need to confirm it, and here's what happens next.'"
