# Claimly Live Demo Script (3 Minutes)

**Presenter:** Masroor (Marketing)  
**Start:** Already logged in  
**Total Time:** 2:45–3:00 minutes  
**Target Audience:** Judges (make it clear, energetic, focused on impact)

---

## SECTION 1: OPENING & DASHBOARD (0:00–0:20)

**[Start on DASHBOARD page - /dashboard]**

**Script:**
"Here's the Claimly dashboard. When users log in, they see a snapshot of their financial help opportunities—saved programs they're tracking, active claims, and all the documents they've uploaded.

But the real magic? Everything starts with one question."

**Action:**
- Show the dashboard cards: "Saved programs," "Active claims," "Documents"
- Highlight the AI assistant banner at the bottom

---

## SECTION 2: CLAIM DISCOVERY (0:20–0:50)

**[Navigate to HOME page - /]**

**Script:**
"Instead of forcing families to navigate confusing government websites or search for programs they don't know about, Claimly brings everything together in one place.

Right here—users describe their situation in one sentence. Their ZIP code. Claimly instantly matches them with programs they actually qualify for."

**Action:**
- Show the hero section with the animated headline
- Point to the search box
- Type in: *"Single parent with 2 kids, $2,200/month income, need help with food and rent"*
- Enter ZIP: *"21218"* (Baltimore)
- Click "Find help"
- Let the search animation play ("Understanding your situation... Checking federal programs...")

---

## SECTION 3: RESULTS & MATCHES (0:50–1:30)

**[Show Results section]**

**Script:**
"In seconds, Claimly shows every program this family likely qualifies for. See that label? 'Strong fit'—that means they're almost definitely eligible.

Each card shows:
- What the program is
- The estimated monthly benefit
- Why it matches their situation

But here's what makes Claimly different—instead of telling them 'go apply on this .gov website,' we let them apply right here. One application. One conversation."

**Action:**
- Scroll through the results cards slowly
- Point to SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, tax credits, etc.
- Highlight the "Why for you" sections
- Click on one strong match (e.g., **SNAP** or **Medicaid**)
- This opens the **ApplyWizard modal**

---

## SECTION 4: SMART APPLICATION WIZARD (1:30–2:15)

**[In ApplyWizard - Intro Stage]**

**Script:**
"Claimly uses AI to guide them through the application conversationally. Instead of a 20-page government form, it asks 4-7 smart questions.

Watch—if they've applied for another program, Claimly remembers their answers. That's the 'Smart Profile.' No re-entering information. Ever."

**Action:**
- Show the intro stage with the program name, description, and remaining questions
- Point to the "Your saved information" section (showing how many fields pre-filled)
- Click "Start application"
- Move through 2-3 questions naturally:
  1. *"Full name"* - already filled (Smart Profile)
  2. *"Household size"* - already filled
  3. *"Monthly income"* - already filled
- For one question, **pause and explain**: "See? Claimly only asks what SNAP actually needs. No SSN. No unnecessary details. Just what matters."

**Script (during questions):**
"Each question is in plain English. Not jargon. This is built for real people—busy parents, elderly on fixed income, anyone. 

And see those fields already filled? They came from their Smart Profile. The more applications they complete with Claimly, the faster each new one gets."

---

## SECTION 5: DOCUMENT UPLOAD & REVIEW (2:15–2:45)

**[Progress through to Review/Preview stage]**

**Script:**
"Once they answer the essential questions, Claimly gathers everything—their household info, income, kids, housing situation. 

Then it reviews it against the actual eligibility rules. If everything looks good, it shows them the eligibility prediction and next steps."

**Action:**
- Show the preview/review stage
- Point to any documents already attached (or mention "upload supporting docs here")
- Show the eligibility summary (e.g., "You likely qualify for $300/month SNAP benefits")
- Highlight the "Submit" button

---

## SECTION 6: CONFIRMATION & TRACKING (2:45–3:00)

**[Click Submit OR navigate back to APPLICATIONS page - /applications]**

**Script:**
"When they submit, Claimly generates a PDF and tracks the application. Everything is in their dashboard:

- Status of each claim
- Estimated monthly benefits
- Hours saved across all applications
- How many fields Claimly auto-filled

No more juggling tabs. No forgetting which program you applied for. One dashboard. Total clarity.

**This is Claimly—transforming how Americans access the help they've already earned.**"

**Action:**
- Show the Applications page with a sample completed application
- Point to the metrics: "3 applications, 12 fields auto-filled, 2 hours saved"
- Show one application card with status, date, buttons to "View PDF," "Download PDF," "Reuse information"

---

## KEY TALKING POINTS (If asked during Q&A)

1. **Privacy:** "All data is encrypted. Users control what info is reused. No data sold."
2. **Cost:** "Completely free. We're working with nonprofits and government agencies."
3. **Who it serves:** "People who don't know what programs exist or can't navigate the bureaucracy. First-generation immigrants. Elderly. Single parents."
4. **AI role:** "GPT-4 generates conversational questions and eligibility assessments. It makes government forms human."
5. **Real impact:** "Average user discovers 5-8 new programs they didn't know existed. Combined annual value: $10,000+."

- **0:20–0:50** - Claim discovery & search (30s)
- **0:50–1:30** - Results & selection (40s)
- **1:30–2:15** - Application wizard & smart questions (45s)
- **2:15–2:45** - Review & document upload (30s)
- **2:45–3:00** - Confirmation & impact closing (15s)

**Total: ~3 minutes exactly**

---

## ENERGY & TONE

✅ **Be conversational.** Not robotic. You're helping judges *see* the product solve a real problem.  
✅ **Slow down for key moments.** Let the app speak for itself visually.  
✅ **Use "we" language.** Show you're part of the mission, not just demoing a tool.  
✅ **End strong.** Leave them thinking about the families who will use this.

---

## CONTINGENCIES

**If the app is slow:**
- Skip ahead to screenshots or a pre-recorded clip of the results page
- Keep talking about the workflow

**If a judge interrupts:**
- Answer briefly, then pivot back: "But let me show you what happens next..."

**If you run long:**
- Cut Section 4 (wizard) to 30 seconds; focus on the dashboard & results instead
